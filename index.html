🦃 Thanksgiving Face-Off

Set things up here before the family is watching (this screen shows all the answers). Then plug into the TV, hit Start, and press F for fullscreen. Everything saves automatically in this browser, so a refresh won't wipe the scores.

Main keys

1–8 reveal an answer
X strike   Z undo strike
B buzzer only (face-offs)
Q points to left team
P points to right team
N next round   ? all keys

Main rounds

One round per block, with a blank line between rounds. First line is the question. Add x2 or x3 at the end of the question for double or triple points. Then one answer per line with its points at the end, like Turkey 36. Up to 8 answers, auto-sorted by points. The sample points are made up, so tweak away.

Fast Money

Same format, 5 questions. These only show on your host sheet and the answer entry screen, not on the TV board.
